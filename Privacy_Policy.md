Stox Junior Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Information We Collect
Stox Junior collects only the information necessary to provide your account and save your progress.
We may collect:
Username
Optional email address (used only for password recovery)
Simulated portfolio information, including virtual trades, balances, achievements, and app progress

Information We Do Not Collect
Stox Junior does not collect:
Real financial or banking information
Payment information
Government identification
Real names (unless voluntarily entered as a username)
Location data
Contacts
Photos or camera access
Microphone recordings
Device advertising identifiers
Usage analytics

How Your Information Is Used
Your information is used only to:
Create and manage your account
Save your simulated portfolio
Restore your account if you provide an email address
Provide app features

Data Storage
Account data is securely stored using Supabase.
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.
Data Security
We use reasonable technical measures to protect your account information. While no online service can guarantee absolute security, we work to keep your information protected.

Children's Privacy
Stox Junior is intended for educational purposes.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 beyond a username and an optional email address for account recovery. No real name, address, phone number, or payment information is requested.
If you believe a child has provided personal information beyond what is described here, please contact us so we can remove it.

Account Deletion
You can permanently delete your account at any time from the Profile tab within the app. This removes your account information and simulated portfolio data from our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app with an updated revision date.
