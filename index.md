# Stox Junior Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: July 26, 2026**

Stox Junior (“Stox Junior,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains what information Stox Junior collects, how that information is used and stored, and the choices available to users.

## 1. Information We Collect

Stox Junior collects only the information necessary to provide and maintain the app’s features.

### Username

Users may create an account using a username. A username is used to identify the user within the app.

### Email Address

Providing an email address is optional. If provided, it may be used for account recovery and other account-related purposes.

### Simulated Portfolio Information

Stox Junior stores information associated with a user's simulated investment activity, including:

* Virtual trades
* Simulated account balances
* Stocks held within the simulated portfolio
* Achievements and related in-app progress

This information is used solely to provide the app's simulated investing and educational features.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

Stox Junior does **not** collect or require users to provide:

* Real financial or payment information
* Bank account or credit card information
* Precise location information
* Contacts
* Camera or microphone access
* Device identifiers
* Analytics or tracking information

Stox Junior does not require access to a user's contacts, camera, microphone, or location to function.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information collected by Stox Junior is used to:

* Create and maintain user accounts
* Provide account recovery when an email address has been provided
* Maintain simulated portfolios and virtual transactions
* Track achievements and in-app progress
* Provide and maintain the educational features of the app

We do not use collected information for targeted advertising or behavioral tracking.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Stox Junior stores user account and simulated portfolio information using **Supabase**.

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 5. Sharing of Information

Stox Junior does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information is used to operate and provide the features of Stox Junior. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking services to monitor users' activity within the app.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Stox Junior is designed as an educational stock-market simulation.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 beyond a username. Stox Junior does not require a child's real name, physical address, telephone number, or other unnecessary personal information to create or use an account.

Because Stox Junior is intended for younger users, we seek to minimize the amount of personal information collected and use information only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to Stox Junior that should not have been collected, they may contact us using the information provided below.

## 7. Simulated Investing Only

Stox Junior is an educational simulation and does **not** involve real money or real financial transactions.

All stocks, prices, balances, trades, and portfolio activity displayed within the app are simulated or fictional. Stox Junior does not provide users with access to real brokerage accounts or financial services.

## 8. Account and Data Deletion

Users may delete their Stox Junior account through the **Profile** section of the app.

When an account is deleted, the associated account and simulated portfolio information will be deleted in accordance with our data-retention practices.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Stox Junior, our practices, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the **“Last Updated”** date at the top of this policy will be updated accordingly. Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
